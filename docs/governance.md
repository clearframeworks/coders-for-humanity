# Governance foundation

The public constitution at `/constitution` defines public benefit, open source, protection from commercial capture, transparency, outcomes, reusability, accessibility, privacy, and sustainability.

This software supports a proposed role model: Board, Institutional Maintainers, Program Stewards, Project Maintainers, Technical Maintainers, Contributors, Domain Advisors, and Reviewers. It does not claim that a board has been appointed or that a legal nonprofit entity has been registered.

Project acceptance requires evidence of need, consideration of existing solutions, a documented scope, appropriate artifact licenses, maintainership, review capacity, privacy and security analysis, a deployment model, and success measures. Research can conclude that software is not appropriate.

Publish decision records with context, options, decision, consequences, responsible reviewers, conflicts, and date. Retain superseded records. Sponsors and labor contributors receive neither exclusive ownership nor automatic governance authority. Recuse conflicted reviewers, record dissent, and provide an appeal to unconflicted institutional stewards.

Institutional roles are provisioned only by a trusted database operator. Public users cannot grant themselves authority. Editorial publication remains operator-managed until a reviewed administration workflow exists. Changes to that boundary require explicit review and tests.

Before public launch, adopt a voting and appointment process, reporting channels, incident and conduct responsibilities, financial controls, and a succession procedure. Publish those decisions rather than presenting this proposed model as already adopted.
